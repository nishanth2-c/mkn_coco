About Our Business

From Farm to Market

MKN COCOTRADES is a coconut trading business founded by Nishanth K. in Omalur, Tamil Nadu, dedicated to creating a smooth, dependable link between agricultural coconut producing regions and commercial market buyers.

Operating on a direct Farmer-to-Buyer model, our mission is built around transparent communication, honest grading, and lasting commercial relationships. Whether you are an independent retail vendor requiring steady batches or a wholesale distributor sourcing significant tonnage, we make the enquiry and fulfillment process straightforward.
